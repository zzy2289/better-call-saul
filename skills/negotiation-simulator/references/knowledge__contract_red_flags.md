# Contract Red Flags

This is not legal advice. It is a practical issue-spotting guide for negotiation and risk review.

## Red flags

### Vague scope

Problem:

- No clear deliverables.
- No acceptance criteria.
- No revision limits.
- No timeline.

Ask for:

- Defined deliverables.
- Number of revision rounds.
- Client response deadlines.
- What counts as completion.

### Payment ambiguity

Problem:

- No due date.
- No late fee.
- No milestone schedule.
- Payment tied to vague satisfaction.

Ask for:

- Deposit.
- Milestone payments.
- Final payment due before source-file transfer.
- Late payment terms.

### Unilateral changes

Problem:

- Other side can change scope, pricing, timing, or terms without consent.

Ask for:

- Written change-order process.
- Mutual approval for scope changes.

### Broad indemnity

Problem:

- User must cover broad losses not caused by the user.

Ask for:

- Narrow indemnity tied to user's breach, negligence, or willful misconduct.

### IP transfer before payment

Problem:

- Client gets ownership before full payment.

Ask for:

- License/ownership transfer only after full cleared payment.

### Auto-renewal and cancellation traps

Problem:

- Renewal without notice.
- Long cancellation windows.
- Early termination penalties.

Ask for:

- Reminder notice.
- Clear cancellation method.
- Pro-rated refund or reasonable termination path.

### Non-compete or exclusivity

Problem:

- Limits future work too broadly.

Ask for:

- Narrow scope, time, geography, and client category.

### One-sided termination

Problem:

- Other side can terminate anytime without paying for work performed.

Ask for:

- Payment for completed work.
- Kill fee.
- Transition period.

## Negotiation line

```text
I am comfortable with the commercial goal, but this clause is too broad as written. I can agree if we narrow it to [specific narrower version].
```

## Saul angle

A bad clause is a future fight hiding in nice formatting. Fix the ambiguity while everyone is still smiling.
