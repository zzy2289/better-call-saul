# China Consumer Rights

## Key laws and channels

### Consumer Protection Law (消费者权益保护法)

- Consumers have the right to return goods within 7 days without reason for online purchases (Article 25, "七天无理由退货").
- Exception: perishable goods, digital content opened/used, custom-made items, newspapers/magazines.
- Goods with quality problems: the consumer may demand repair, replacement, or refund regardless of the 7-day window.

### Three Guarantees (三包规定)

- Repair, replacement, or return within defined periods for certain product categories.
- Typical: 7 days for return, 15 days for exchange, product-dependent warranty for repair.

### E-commerce Law (电子商务法)

- Platforms must display seller identity, contact, and return policy.
- Platforms bear joint liability when they know or should know a seller is infringing consumer rights.

## Complaint channels

### 12315

- National consumer complaint hotline and online platform.
- Online: [12315.cn](https://www.12315.cn) or the 12315 WeChat mini-program.
- Effective for: product quality, false advertising, refund refusal, delivery disputes.
- Response time: usually 7 business days for acceptance, 60 days for resolution.
- Tip: filing a 12315 complaint is free and often triggers seller cooperation because platforms monitor complaint records.

### 12345

- Government service hotline, can route to consumer protection.
- Useful as a backup or when 12315 does not cover the issue.

### Platform internal complaint

- Taobao/Tmall: 淘宝小蜜 → 投诉 → 平台介入.
- JD (京东): 客服 → 投诉 → 交易纠纷.
- Pinduoduo (拼多多): 官方客服 → 投诉.
- Douyin (抖音电商): 售后 → 平台介入.
- Most platforms have a "仅退款" (refund only) option for certain disputes.

### Court / arbitration

- Small claims: simplified procedure in local People's Court, usually under ¥50,000.
- Online courts available in some cities (互联网法院).

## Strong angles

### False advertising (虚假宣传)

```text
商品实物与商品页面描述严重不符，构成虚假宣传。根据《消费者权益保护法》第五十五条，经营者提供商品或服务有欺诈行为的，应当按照消费者的要求增加赔偿其受到的损失，增加赔偿的金额为消费者购买商品的价款或接受服务的费用的三倍（退一赔三）。
```

### Refund refusal after 7-day window dispute

```text
本人在收货后7日内已申请退货退款，商家以[理由]拒绝。根据《消法》第二十五条，网购商品7日内无理由退货是法定权利。请平台介入处理。
```

### Quality defect

```text
收到商品存在[具体质量问题]，属于不符合质量要求的商品。根据《消法》第二十四条，我有权要求退货、更换或修理，且由经营者承担运费等必要费用。
```

## 12315 complaint template

```text
投诉对象：[商家名称/店铺名]
购买渠道：[平台名称]
订单编号：[编号]
购买日期：[日期]
商品名称：[名称]
投诉事由：[具体问题，如"商品与描述不符""拒绝退款""虚假宣传"等]
诉求：[退款/退一赔三/换货/赔偿]
已有证据：[订单截图、聊天记录、商品照片、物流信息等]
```

## Escalation ladder (China-specific)

1. Platform customer service (客服).
2. Platform complaint / dispute resolution (投诉/平台介入).
3. 12315 complaint (消费者投诉).
4. 12345 hotline (政务服务热线).
5. Media exposure / social media (媒体曝光 / 社交媒体).
6. Court (法院起诉).

## Saul angle

In China e-commerce disputes, the magic words are "12315" and "退一赔三" (triple damages). Most sellers settle fast once they see a formal complaint because platforms penalize shops with high complaint rates. File the 12315 complaint first, then message the seller saying you have filed — this often unlocks cooperation within hours.
