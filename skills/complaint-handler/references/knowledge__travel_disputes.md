# Travel Disputes

## Common scenarios

- Hotel refuses refund after service failure.
- Hotel overbooks and moves guest to worse property.
- Airline delay or cancellation.
- OTA points to hotel/airline while hotel/airline points back.
- Non-refundable booking affected by seller-side failure.
- Hidden fees.
- Room not as advertised.
- Cancellation policy unclear or misrepresented.

## Evidence checklist

- Booking confirmation.
- Cancellation policy shown at booking.
- Photos of room/property issue.
- Messages with hotel, airline, or OTA.
- Check-in/out timeline.
- Receipts for replacement accommodation or transport.
- Names/ticket numbers.

## Split responsibility

Travel disputes often involve multiple parties:

- Hotel/airline/service provider.
- OTA/booking platform.
- Payment provider.
- Travel insurer.

Do not let each side bounce the user endlessly. Ask each party for its exact basis for refusal and the party responsible for remedy.

## OTA escalation template

```text
I booked through your platform, paid through your checkout, and relied on the accommodation details presented at purchase. The property did not provide what was sold because [issue].

Please either process [refund/credit/rebooking] or confirm in writing why your buyer/guest protection process does not apply.
```

## Hotel overbooking template

```text
The hotel could not honor the confirmed booking and moved me to [alternative], which was materially worse because [facts]. I am requesting [refund/price difference/reimbursement] based on the failure to provide the confirmed accommodation.
```

## Airline caution

Air passenger rights vary heavily by country, route, airline, and cause of delay. Do not make definitive legal claims unless the applicable jurisdiction is known.

Use general wording:

```text
Please review this under the passenger rights rules applicable to this itinerary and provide the written basis for your decision.
```

## Saul angle

The travel industry loves the blame triangle. Break it by asking each party to put its refusal and responsibility position in writing.
