# Subscription Cancellation and Refund Friction

## Common scenarios

- User cannot find cancellation button.
- Trial converts without clear reminder.
- Subscription renews after cancellation attempt.
- Support delays cancellation.
- SaaS refuses refund after non-use.
- App store, merchant, and payment provider shift responsibility.

## Evidence checklist

- Signup date.
- Renewal date.
- Cancellation attempts.
- Screenshots of cancellation flow.
- Support messages.
- Terms shown at purchase.
- Usage logs if relevant.
- Payment receipt.

## Strong angles

### Cancellation attempt before renewal

```text
I attempted to cancel on [date] before renewal, but the cancellation flow/support process did not complete it. I am requesting a refund of the renewal charge and confirmation that the subscription is canceled.
```

### No meaningful use after renewal

```text
The renewal occurred on [date], and there has been no meaningful use since then. I am requesting a one-time refund and immediate cancellation.
```

### Dark pattern/friction framing

Use carefully and factually.

```text
The cancellation path was not clear, and my documented attempts did not result in cancellation. Please review this as a billing support issue rather than a standard refund exception.
```

## Cancellation script

```text
Please cancel my subscription immediately and confirm the effective cancellation date in writing. I am also requesting a refund for the [date] charge because [reason].

If you cannot approve the refund, please provide the exact policy section you are relying on and escalate this to billing support.
```

## Payment provider caution

A payment dispute can be appropriate if the merchant charged after a valid cancellation or failed to provide the purchased service. It is not appropriate when the user simply forgot to cancel and the terms were clear.

## Saul angle

Do not argue about whether you "liked" the service. Argue the clean billing issue: cancellation attempt, renewal timing, lack of use, and written confirmation.
