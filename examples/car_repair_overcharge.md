# Example: Car repair shop overcharged and did unnecessary work

## User scenario

I took my car in for a brake inspection. The shop called and said I also needed new rotors, a transmission fluid flush, and an engine air filter — total $1,800. I approved the brakes and filter ($600) but not the other work. When I picked up the car, the bill was $1,650 because they did the transmission flush anyway, saying it was "critical for safety." I have the original estimate, my approval message (text) for only two items, and the final invoice. I want to pay only for the work I approved.

Use `complaint-handler` and `angle-finder`.
