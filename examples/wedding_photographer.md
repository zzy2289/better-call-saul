# Example: Wedding photographer delivered poor quality photos

## User scenario

I paid a wedding photographer $3,500 for full-day coverage. The contract promised 400+ edited photos within 6 weeks. I received 280 photos after 10 weeks, and at least 50 of them are blurry, poorly lit, or have bad cropping. Key moments like the first dance and cake cutting are missing entirely. The photographer says the lighting was difficult and this is their artistic style. I have the contract, sample portfolio they showed me before booking, the delivered photos, and screenshots of our pre-wedding communication about must-have shots. I want a partial refund.

## Desired output

Use `negotiation-simulator` and `angle-finder`.
