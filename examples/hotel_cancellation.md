# Example: Hotel overbooking and worse replacement

## User scenario

I booked a hotel through an online travel agency. When I arrived, the hotel said they were overbooked and moved me to another property farther away with a smaller room and no breakfast. The OTA says the hotel is responsible. The hotel says I need to deal with the OTA. I want a refund for the price difference and the inconvenience.

## Desired output

Use `complaint-handler` and `negotiation-simulator`.

Expected angle:

- Confirmed booking was not honored.
- Replacement was materially worse.
- OTA sold and processed the booking.
- Ask both parties for written basis if they deny responsibility.
