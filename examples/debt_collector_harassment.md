# Example: Debt collector harassment for an old medical bill

## User scenario

A collection agency has been calling me three to four times a day about a medical bill from 2019 for $1,800. I do not remember this bill and I think my insurance should have covered it. They call my cell phone before 8am and have also called my workplace. When I asked them to validate the debt, they said they would send documentation but never did. The calls have continued for three weeks. I want them to stop calling and prove I actually owe this.

## Desired output

Use `angle-finder` and `complaint-handler`.
