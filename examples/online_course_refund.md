# Example: Online course refund refused after misleading marketing

## User scenario

I bought an online course for $497 that advertised "learn to build a SaaS product and make $10k/month." The sales page had income testimonials and a money-back guarantee within 14 days. I went through the first four modules in three days and found the content was basic tutorials freely available on YouTube. I requested a refund on day 5. They refused, saying the guarantee requires completing all 12 modules and submitting a "proof of effort" document first. This requirement was not on the sales page — only in a terms PDF linked in tiny text at the bottom. I want a full refund.

Use `complaint-handler` and `angle-finder`.
