# Example: Laptop warranty denied due to alleged misuse

## User scenario

My laptop's screen started flickering after eight months. The manufacturer warranty covers defects for one year. I sent it in for repair, and they denied the claim saying there was evidence of "liquid damage" on the motherboard. I have never spilled anything on the laptop. The flickering is a known issue for this model — there are forum posts from hundreds of other users with the same defect. I want a free repair or replacement.

## Desired output

Use `complaint-handler` and `angle-finder`.
