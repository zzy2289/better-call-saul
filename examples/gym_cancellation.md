# Example: Gym membership cancellation refusal

## User scenario

I signed up for a gym membership online. The contract says I can cancel with 30 days notice. I sent a cancellation email 45 days ago and got a confirmation reply. They charged me again this month. When I called, they said the cancellation was not processed because I did not fill out a separate paper form at the front desk. The paper form requirement is not mentioned in the online contract I signed. I want the latest charge refunded and the membership cancelled immediately.

## Desired output

Use `complaint-handler` and `angle-finder`.
