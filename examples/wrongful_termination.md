# Example: Wrongful termination with suspected retaliation

## User scenario

I reported a safety violation at my workplace to HR two months ago. Last week I was terminated, and my manager cited "performance issues." My performance reviews from the past two years have all been positive, and I received a raise six months ago. I believe the termination is retaliation for reporting the safety issue. I have copies of my reviews, the raise confirmation email, my safety report submission, and the termination letter. I want severance and a clean reference.

## Desired output

Use `negotiation-simulator` and `risk-assessor`.
