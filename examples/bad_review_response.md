# Example: Bad review response

## User scenario

A customer left a one-star review saying my small business ignored them and overcharged them. I checked records and they received the quoted service, but we did take too long to respond to one email. I want to reply publicly without starting a fight or admitting too much.

## Desired output

Use `risk-assessor` and `angle-finder`.

Expected angle:

- Acknowledge delay.
- Do not argue every detail publicly.
- Invite private follow-up.
- Sound reasonable for future readers.
