# Example: Contractor did not finish renovation and demands full payment

## User scenario

I hired a contractor to renovate my kitchen for $15,000. We agreed on a timeline of six weeks. After four weeks, they completed about 60% of the work, then stopped showing up. They are now demanding full payment, saying the remaining work is "minor finishing" that was not in the original scope. I have the signed contract with a detailed scope of work, progress photos, and text messages where they acknowledged the remaining tasks. I want the work finished or a proportional refund.

## Desired output

Use `negotiation-simulator` and `angle-finder`.
