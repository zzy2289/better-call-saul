# Example: Credit card chargeback for undelivered goods

## User scenario

I ordered a custom desk from a small online furniture store for $850. They promised delivery in 4-6 weeks. It has now been 12 weeks. I have emailed them five times — they replied once saying it was delayed, then stopped responding. Their phone number goes to voicemail. I paid with a Visa credit card. I want my money back.

## Desired output

Use `complaint-handler` and `risk-assessor`.
