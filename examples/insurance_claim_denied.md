# Example: Insurance claim denied after car accident

## User scenario

My car was hit while parked in a lot. The other driver left a note with their insurance info. I filed a claim with their insurer. After two weeks, they denied it saying the damage was "pre-existing." The car was in perfect condition before — I have photos from the day before the incident and the body shop estimate. The denial letter does not cite any specific policy language. I want them to cover the $3,200 repair.
