# Example: Subscription renewal after cancellation attempt

## User scenario

I tried to cancel a SaaS subscription before renewal, but the cancellation button kept sending me to a support form. Support replied after the renewal date and said they do not offer refunds. I have screenshots from before renewal and the support ticket date. I want the renewal refunded and the account canceled.

## Desired output

Use `complaint-handler`, `angle-finder`, and `risk-assessor`.

Expected angle:

- Cancellation attempt before renewal.
- Cancellation process failed or delayed.
- Request immediate cancellation, refund, and written confirmation.
- Ask for exact policy basis if refused.
