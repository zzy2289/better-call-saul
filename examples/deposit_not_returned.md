# Example: Security deposit not returned after move-out

## User scenario

I moved out of my apartment two months ago. The landlord has not returned my $2,400 security deposit and has not provided an itemized list of deductions. I left the apartment in the same condition as move-in except for normal wear. I have timestamped move-in and move-out photos, my lease agreement, and a copy of the move-out inspection we signed together. The local law requires the deposit to be returned within 30 days. I want my full deposit back.

## Desired output

Use `complaint-handler` and `angle-finder`.
