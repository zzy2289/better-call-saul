# Example: Airline lost baggage and inadequate compensation

## User scenario

My airline lost my checked bag on a connecting flight from London to Tokyo. It has been 10 days and they still cannot locate it. The bag contained clothes, electronics, and work documents worth roughly $2,500. The airline offered a $200 interim allowance and says they will pay up to $400 total if the bag is declared lost after 21 days. I think this is too low. I have the baggage claim receipt, my ticket, and a rough inventory of what was in the bag. I want fair compensation.

## Desired output

Use `complaint-handler` and `angle-finder`.
