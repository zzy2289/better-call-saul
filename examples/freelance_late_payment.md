# Example: Freelance late payment and scope creep

## User scenario

A client hired me to build a landing page for $1,200 with two revision rounds. They added two extra sections, delayed feedback for three weeks, then said the project missed their launch date and they only want to pay 50 percent. I delivered the work on time based on the original schedule and have messages showing the extra requests and delayed feedback.

## Desired output

Use `angle-finder`, `negotiation-simulator`, and `risk-assessor`.

Expected angle:

- Re-anchor to agreed scope and delivery.
- Separate extra work from original invoice.
- Request full payment for completed scope.
- Offer add-on pricing for extra sections.
- Pause new work until overdue invoice is paid.
