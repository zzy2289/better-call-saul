# Example: Neighbor dispute over property boundary and fence

## User scenario

My neighbor built a new fence that encroaches about two feet onto my property. I pointed this out with my property survey document. They say the fence follows the "existing boundary" and refuse to move it. They have also started parking their car so it blocks part of my driveway. I have the property survey, photos of the fence placement, and photos of the blocked driveway. I want the fence moved to the correct boundary and the driveway kept clear.

## Desired output

Use `negotiation-simulator` and `risk-assessor`.
